Thank you for the review. I have uploaded version 0.1.30, which addresses the issues raised; the Plugin URI repository is now public.

On the crowded category: Hatnikotni Chat is meant as an enquiry-routing tool for small teams rather than a social share button. It distributes WhatsApp enquiries automatically across several staff numbers (round robin, with a fallback if rotation cannot be updated), and shows which pages and campaigns lead to enquiries using first-party click records kept in the site's own database. Those records are made only after the visitor accepts analytics, and WhatsApp keeps working if they decline.

Among the most-used WhatsApp plugins I checked (Joinchat, Click to Chat, Social Chat, Chaty, WP Chat App), several numbers are mostly a premium feature, I could not find automatic round-robin distribution, and click tracking is sent to Google Analytics or Meta Pixel rather than kept locally behind visitor consent.
